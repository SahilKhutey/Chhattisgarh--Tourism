# MV11: Pilot Framework, Scale Readiness & Market Launch Execution

## 1. Executive Summary

MV11 converts the empirical findings and governing authority of **MV10 (Go / Conditional Decision)** into an operational execution and scale-readiness system for CG Tourism OS.

Rather than conducting additional broad consumer surveys, MV11 establishes:
1. **Single Circuit Pilot Definition**: Bastar Tribal Heritage Circuit 1 (Chitrakote Falls, Tirathgarh Falls, Kanger Valley National Park, Dholkal Ganesh).
2. **Product Scope Guardrails**: Strict enforcement of in-scope features (discovery, itineraries, qualified WhatsApp/SMS lead dispatch) vs out-of-scope boundaries (statewide unrestricted marketplace, custodial escrow, unvetted adventure sports).
3. **12-Gate Launch Readiness Assessment**: Mandatory pre-launch certification across technical, operational, and safety checkpoints.
4. **Automated Launch Controls & Circuit Breakers**: Real-time safeguards including traffic caps, lead queue limits, and critical incident safety disables.
5. **Operational Capacity & Founder Decoupling**: Tracking support desk utilization, field turnaround SLAs, and ensuring founder intervention rate stays under 20%.
6. **10-Gate Scale Governance**: Defensible decision logic mapping failure modes to `STOP`, `PAUSE`, `PIVOT`, `CONTINUE_PILOT`, `LIMITED_EXPANSION`, or `SCALE`.
7. **Expansion Candidate Modeling**: Algorithmic transferability scoring to replicate proven Bastar playbooks into Surguja, Bilaspur, and Raipur.

## 3. 12-Gate Launch Readiness Assessment

1. **Product Core**: Discovery and itinerary engine validated.
2. **Content Coverage**: Verified Bastar destination profiles $\ge 85\%$.
3. **Geographic Data**: GPS coordinates, routing durations, and elevation verified.
4. **Supply Activation**: Minimum 15 vetted local homestays and guides active.
5. **Consumer Demand**: Pre-launch waitlist and organic search capture online.
6. **Transaction Handoff**: Verified lead dispatch to provider WhatsApp/SMS active.
7. **Analytics Pipeline**: Telemetry recording cohorts, funnel stages, and drop-offs.
8. **Support Desk**: 2 dedicated support agents covering 08:00 - 20:00 IST.
9. **Security Hardening**: RBAC enforced; JWT and rate limits armed.
10. **Privacy Compliance**: Zero PII leakage in telemetry or crash reports.
11. **Safety Protocols**: Emergency medical/police dispatch escalation armed.
12. **Ops & Monitoring**: Uptime alarms, error budgets, and database health probes active.

## 4. 10 Post-Pilot Scale Gates

- **Gate 1 (Consumer Demand)**: Discovery-to-plan conversion $\ge 20\%$.
- **Gate 2 (Supply Activation)**: Active participating providers $\ge 15$.
- **Gate 3 (Transaction Feasibility)**: Qualified lead conversion $\ge 15\%$.
- **Gate 4 (Unit Economics)**: Contribution margin $> 10\%$.
- **Gate 5 (Operational Stability)**: Unresolved incident rate $< 2\%$.
- **Gate 6 (Safety Compliance)**: Zero critical unaddressed safety breaches (**Hard Blocker**).
- **Gate 7 (Content Completeness)**: Verified regional coverage $\ge 85\%$.
- **Gate 8 (Retention Intent)**: Trip cycle repeat/recommend intent $\ge 40\%$.
- **Gate 9 (Founder Decoupling)**: Founder manual workflow intervention $< 20\%$.
- **Gate 10 (Market Transferability)**: Candidate expansion affinity score $\ge 60$.
