# MV3: Tourism Supply-Side Validation Research Protocol

## 1. Objectives & Guiding Principle

MV3 validates the supply side of the CG Tourism OS marketplace:
> *“Will tourism businesses, homestays, and local experience providers participate, maintain their information, respond to demand, and perceive enough economic value to justify future monetization?”*

### The Golden Supply Validation Rule
**Do not ask providers hypothetically if they would like more tourists.** Every provider will answer "yes."
Instead, observe and test:
1. **Actual operational behavior**: How do they currently receive, process, and confirm inquiries?
2. **Maintenance willingness**: Will they take the 5–10 minutes to submit photos, prices, coordinates, and contact details?
3. **Response latency**: When real or qualified traveler demand is dispatched to their phone/WhatsApp, how fast do they acknowledge and respond?
4. **Economic threshold**: When a booking is completed, what value do they attribute to the platform, and what fee structure (commission per booking vs subscription) aligns with their cash flow?

## 3. Five Validation Experiments

### Experiment 1: Provider Onboarding Friction Test
- **Purpose**: Measure drop-off rate across the 7 onboarding steps.
- **Protocol**: Guide provider through the 7 steps via mobile web interface. Record time-to-onboard and questions causing hesitation.
- **Success Criteria**: >60% completion rate for self-directed or concierge-assisted onboarding in under 12 minutes.

### Experiment 2: Listing Quality & Completeness Test
- **Purpose**: Determine if providers provide sufficient information (coordinates, authentic photos, verified phone numbers) to publish.
- **Criteria**: Listing quality score $\ge 50/100$, with mandatory non-zero location and contact scores. Listings missing coordinates are strictly blocked from publishing.

### Experiment 3: Lead Dispatch & Response Velocity Test
- **Purpose**: Measure provider responsiveness to qualified traveler inquiries.
- **Protocol**: Dispatched verified traveler leads via WhatsApp/SMS link. Record timestamp of response.
- **Target SLA**:
  - Homestays & Guides: Median response time $\le 2$ hours.
  - Tour Operators: Median response time $\le 1$ hour.
  - Artisan Workshops: Handled via concierge proxy within 24 hours.

### Experiment 4: Conversion & Economic Delivery Test
- **Purpose**: Verify end-to-end booking conversion without building premature payment infrastructure.
- **Metric**: Confirmed bookings / qualified leads (target $>25\%$).

### Experiment 5: Post-Service Feedback & Willingness to Pay (WTP)
- **Purpose**: Gauge perceived economic value and monetization readiness.
- **Methodology**: Administer feedback survey after first 3 traveler bookings. Assess preference between:
  - Option A: Free listing + 8–12% commission per confirmed booking.
  - Option B: Monthly subscription fee (₹500–₹1,500/mo).
  - Option C: Premium search placement / sponsored highlights.

---

## 4. Ethical & Local Community Safeguards
- **Informed Consent**: Providers must understand how their contact information is shared with travelers.
- **No Exploitation**: Zero fees are charged to tribal artisans or community homestays during the validation phase.
- **Direct Relationships**: CG Tourism acts as an open protocol and demand generator, never withholding customer contact info from local providers.
