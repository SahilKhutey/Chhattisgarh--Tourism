# MV5 — Content & Discovery Jobs-To-Be-Done (JTBD)

In accordance with the foundational rule of CG Tourism OS market validation:
*Do not ask travelers if they like articles; measure how efficiently content resolves their active jobs.*

---

## 1. Primary Traveler Jobs

### JTBD-1: Rapid Viability Assessment
> *"When I find an intriguing destination online, I want to quickly know whether it is accessible, safe, and appropriate for my travel group (family, solo, elders) so I don't waste hours researching a place that isn't feasible."*

- **Friction in Status Quo:** Long unstructured blogs burying ticket prices, road accessibility, and closing times under poetic adjectives.
- **CG Tourism Solution:** 30-second Key Facts Block: Exact road condition, accessibility grade, operating hours, ticket fees, nearest medical/fuel station.
- **Validation Metric:** Time-to-decision & bounce reduction.

---

### JTBD-2: Frictionless Circuit Sequencing
> *"When I decide to visit an anchor destination (e.g. Chitrakote Falls), I want to know what else I can realistically combine on the same day without backtracking or getting stuck on unsafe unpaved roads after dark."*

- **Friction in Status Quo:** Google Maps distances misleading travelers on single-lane forest roads; missing contextual knowledge of combined circuits.
- **CG Tourism Solution:** Embedded 3-Stop Day Circuits: Calculated with real local driving speeds and forest gate closure buffers.
- **Validation Metric:** Second-destination click-through rate ($\ge 60\%$).

---

### JTBD-3: Ground-Truth Truthfulness Verification
> *"When I travel to remote regions of Chhattisgarh, I want to be 100% certain that boating is open, guides are available, and permits aren't needed, so my family is not stranded or disappointed after a 6-hour drive."*

- **Friction in Status Quo:** 3-year-old Quora threads and outdated TripAdvisor reviews claiming attractions are closed or dangerous.
- **CG Tourism Solution:** Verified Provenance Ledger with explicit verifier role and timestamp ($<90$ days freshness).
- **Validation Metric:** Content bookmark rate and verified badge trust lift.

---

### JTBD-4: Cultural & Community Reverence
> *"When I visit indigenous hamlets, sacred groves (Devgudis), or tribal weekly Haats, I want clear guidance on respectful conduct, photography etiquette, and authentic artisan support so I travel as a welcome guest rather than an intrusive tourist."*

- **Friction in Status Quo:** Exploitative photography, confusion over sacred boundaries, purchase of fake souvenirs from middlemen.
- **CG Tourism Solution:** Cultural Etiquette Notes & Verified Cooperative Direct Sales attribution.
- **Validation Metric:** Community elder sign-off rate and zero dispute reports.

---

## 2. Content Progression Matrix

| Traveler Phase | Traveler Job | Content Artifact | Primary Success Event |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Inspiration / Search** | Find high-appeal destinations matching vibe | Thematic Intent Snippet | Content Impression $\to$ Open |
