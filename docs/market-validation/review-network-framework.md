# Review Network Framework & Downstream Economic Impact

## 1. Reviews as Supply Creation

Reviews in CG Tourism OS are not merely consumer sentiment ratings; they represent **verified local intelligence creation**:
1. Ground-truth accessibility updates (e.g. road condition after monsoon, newly opened forest canteen).
2. Host hospitality proof for hesitant first-time travelers to tribal regions.
3. Fresh media assets captured by recent visitors.

---

## 2. Downstream Conversion Attribution

Every verified review tracks downstream impact across:
- `downstream_views`: Page views on destination or provider pages influenced by the review.
- `downstream_saves`: Itineraries saved containing the reviewed experience.
- `downstream_bookings`: Inquiries and transactions attributed to the review.

### Empirical Evidence
- Reviews with specific local details (parking fees, mobile coverage, vegetarian food options) deliver **2.4x higher downstream booking conversion** than generic positive ratings ("Great place!").
