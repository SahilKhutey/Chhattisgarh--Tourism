# Consumer Economics & Monetization Potential

## 1. Consumer Value Proposition
Travelers in Chhattisgarh encounter significant friction in remote circuits (connectivity gaps, permit ambiguity, unverified road accessibility).

### Zero-Paywall Discovery Mandate
Public travel discovery is unmonetized:
- **Search & Filtering**: Free.
- **District Guides (33 Districts)**: Free.
- **Safety Warnings & Weather Advisories**: Free.
- **Standard Community Directory**: Free.

## 2. Value-Add Consumer Products
Monetization is optional and value-add:
1. **Artisan & Cultural Trail Digital Pass**: ₹149 / pass
   - Offline multimedia audio tours for tribal art villages.
   - 70% revenue share to artisan cooperatives, 30% platform margin.
2. **Offline Topographic Route Pack**: ₹99 / route
   - High-resolution GPS trail maps for remote trekking in Kanger Valley and Mainpat.

## 3. Consumer Unit Economics
- **Consumer Spend (SEO & syndication)**: ₹8,000 for 1,600 users.
- **Acquisition CAC**: ₹5.00.
- **Paid Conversion**: 4.1% (65 buyers out of 1,600).
- **Paying CAC**: ₹123.
- **Average Cycles**: 2.5 travel cycles.
- **Consumer LTV**: ₹341.
- **LTV / CAC**: **2.77x**.
