## 3. Workflow Fragmentation Baseline: Task `TASK_BASTAR_3DAY_PLAN`

Researchers gave participants the live task:
> *"Plan a 3-day trip in Bastar starting from Raipur with whatever tools you normally use."*

### Empirical Observations:
- **Average Distinct Tools Used:** $6.4$ (Google Search $\rightarrow$ YouTube $\rightarrow$ Google Maps $\rightarrow$ MakeMyTrip $\rightarrow$ Instagram $\rightarrow$ WhatsApp $\rightarrow$ Apple Notes).
- **Average Duration to First Working Route:** $23.4$ minutes.
- **Average Manual Steps / Copy-Pastes:** $11.2$ steps.
- **Task Abandonment / Compromise Rate:** $58\%$ of participants abandoned their original ambition of visiting remote tribal craft clusters due to logistical anxiety, opting for generic Raipur-Jagdalpur hotel stays instead.
