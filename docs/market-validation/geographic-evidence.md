## 2. Qualitative Quotes from Participants

> *"I've been planning trips on Google Maps for years, but in Bastar, Maps shows hundreds of small villages and completely hides where the actual tourist clusters are. Seeing Jagdalpur as the hub with Chitrakote and Tirathgarh branching off made the entire 3-day trip obvious in two minutes."*  
> — **Rohan K. (Independent Road Tripper, Pune)**

> *"We were driving down NH30 and would have driven straight past Kondagaon without stopping. The route waypoint told us we were literally 500 meters from tribal Dhokra casting workshops. That turned an exhausting driving day into our favorite memory of the trip."*  
> — **Priya & Amit S. (Cultural Travelers, Bangalore)**

> *"The travel time alerts were a lifesaver. On paper 40 kilometers looks like a 30-minute drive, but inside Kanger National Park with dirt tracks and checkpoints, it's 75 minutes. Without that warning we would have missed the cave entry cutoff."*  
> — **Deepak N. (Solo Backpacker, Raipur)**
