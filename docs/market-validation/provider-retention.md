# Provider Retention, Continuation, & Reactivation

## 1. Defining Supply Retention

A provider is defined as `ProviderActive` when they perform at least one of the following within 30 days:
- Update listing inventory, seasonal photos, or tariffs.
- Respond to an inquiry or lead.
- Manage an active booking intent.
- Review platform demand analytics.

---

## 2. The Provider Reactivation Phenomenon

In seasonal tourism, providers often experience off-season dormancy (e.g., peak summer in Bastar).

**Reactivation Event**:
```
Provider inactive for > 30 days
       ↓
Pre-season platform notification (Upcoming Dussehra / Winter festival demand)
       ↓
Provider logs in & updates availability
       ↓
Accepts first new inquiry
       ↓
Recorded as REACTIVATED
```

Reactivation is a strong validator of supply lock-in: local operators view the platform as their dependable tap for tourism demand when travel season resumes.
