# Economic Value Metrics & GTV Facilitation

## 1. Defining Gross Tourism Value (GTV) Facilitated

In traditional digital marketing, value is measured in clicks, impressions, or bounce rates.
In **CG Tourism OS**, market success is defined strictly by **economic value generated for Chhattisgarh**:

$$\text{Total GTV Facilitated} = \sum_{\text{completed transactions}} \text{transaction amount (INR)}$$

This represents direct income delivered to:
- Rural tribal homestay hosts in Bastar and Surguja.
- Local licensed eco-guides and cave navigators in Kanger Valley.
- Traditional craft artisans (Dhokra metal casters, terracotta sculptors).
- Regional rural transportation and boat operators.

---

## 2. Average Transaction Value (ATV) Benchmark

$$\text{ATV} = \frac{\text{Total GTV Facilitated}}{\text{Count of Completed Transactions}}$$

Validation targets across Chhattisgarh circuits:
- **Weekend Getaways (Central / Raipur-Sirpur)**: ₹2,500 - ₹4,500 per booking.
- **Deep Cultural Circuits (Bastar / Kondagaon / Dantewada)**: ₹6,000 - ₹14,000 per booking.
- **Nature / Trekking Expeditions (Mainpat / Surguja)**: ₹4,000 - ₹9,000 per booking.

---

## 3. The Provider Value Score (PVS) Heuristic

To objectively evaluate whether local providers derive genuine business value from participating in CG Tourism OS, the system computes the **Provider Value Score** (0 to 100):

$$\text{PVS} = (w_1 \cdot \text{Response Rate}) + (w_2 \cdot \text{SLA Compliance}) + (w_3 \cdot \text{Conversion Rate}) + (w_4 \cdot \text{Satisfaction Score})$$

Where:
- $w_1 = 0.25$: Percentage of leads responded to.
- $w_2 = 0.25$: Responses delivered within the under-2h SLA window.
- $w_3 = 0.30$: Ratio of qualified leads converted to confirmed bookings.
- $w_4 = 0.20$: Post-trip guest satisfaction rating (scaled to 100).

A PVS score > 75 indicates a healthy, highly motivated provider thriving on platform demand.
A PVS score < 40 flags supply operational distress requiring direct field researcher assistance.
