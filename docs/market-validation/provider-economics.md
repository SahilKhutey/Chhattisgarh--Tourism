# Provider Economics & Profitability

## 1. Provider Value Realization
Rural and tribal tourism providers operate on low liquidity but attractive unit margins per guest:
- **Average Homestay Tariff**: ₹1,500 – ₹2,500 per room night.
- **Average Guest Stay**: 2.2 nights (~₹4,200 per booking).
- **Direct Variable Cost**: ₹800 (food, cleaning, firewood).
- **Net Host Margin**: ~₹3,400 per guest party.

## 2. Lead Fee ROI
When a provider pays **₹25** for a verified lead:
- Lead-to-booking conversion rate: ~35% (due to pre-verified mobile, dates, and budget).
- Expected cost per booked reservation: $\frac{₹25}{0.35} \approx ₹71.40$.
- Effective acquisition cost for host: **1.7% of booking revenue**.
- Compared to OTA commissions (18–25% on MakeMyTrip / Booking.com), CG Tourism OS saves providers **over 90% in distribution costs**.

## 3. Provider Retention & LTV
- **Average Active Lifespan**: 12 active months per annual tourism cycle.
- **Average Monthly Lead Purchases**: 28 leads = ₹700 monthly spend.
- **Average Monthly Contribution**: ₹625 net platform margin.
- **Provider LTV**: $\text{LTV} = ₹625 \times 12 = \mathbf{₹7,500}$.
- **Provider CAC**: **₹300** (field onboarding + verification audit).
- **LTV / CAC**: **12.5x**.
