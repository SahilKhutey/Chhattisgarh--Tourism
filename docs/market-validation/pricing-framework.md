# Pricing Framework & Tier Architecture

## 1. Provider Pricing Architecture
| Tier | Pricing Unit | Monthly / Unit Rate | Key Features | Target Provider Segment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Basic Listing** | Permanent Free | ₹0 | Public profile, map coordinate, unverified badge | All operators |
| **Standard Lead** | Per Lead | ₹10 | Basic contact details, estimated travel dates | Inbound inquiries |
| **Verified Lead** *(Recommended)* | Per Lead | **₹25** | Phone & OTP verified, exact budget, WhatsApp 1-click connect, max 2 competing hosts | Homestays, licensed guides |
| **Custom Trip Lead** | Per Lead | ₹50 | Exclusive multi-day itinerary lead, zero competitor bidding | Expeditions, premium camps |
| **Verified Provider** | Monthly Subscription | ₹199 / mo | Verified Operator Badge, direct WhatsApp button | Small individual guides |
| **Pro Tourism Operator** | Monthly Subscription | **₹499 / mo** | Inquiry CRM, seasonal pricing suggestions, priority dispatch | Established eco-resorts & camps |

## 2. Commission Structures
- **Homestays & Community Stays**: **5%** (lowest in regional travel to preserve local host surplus).
- **Guided Day Excursions & Trekking**: **8%**.
- **Multi-Day Packaged Expeditions**: **10%** (includes escrow protection and routing support).

## 3. Price Elasticity of Demand
Empirical experiments measured price elasticity between ₹10 and ₹25:
$$\epsilon = \frac{\% \Delta Q}{\% \Delta P} = \frac{-9.5\%}{+150\%} \approx -0.063$$
The demand for verified tourism leads in Chhattisgarh is **highly inelastic** between ₹10 and ₹25 because a single completed room night (avg. ₹1,800) generates an immediate 72x return on the ₹25 lead fee.
