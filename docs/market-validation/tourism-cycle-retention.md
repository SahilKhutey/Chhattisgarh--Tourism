# Tourism-Cycle Retention & Next-Trip Metrics

## 1. Mathematical Formulation

### A. Trip-Cycle Retention Rate

$$\text{Trip Cycle Retention} = \frac{\text{Users returning for another meaningful tourism task}}{\text{Users who completed their first meaningful tourism journey}}$$

Where a completed journey requires:
- Initial discovery or planning
- Booking verification or on-ground visit confirmation
- Post-trip evaluation or return session

### B. Next-Trip Rate

$$\text{Next Trip Rate} = \frac{\text{Users creating a second trip / itinerary}}{\text{Users completing their first trip}}$$

This metric measures long-term brand equity and platform trust far more accurately than monthly active users.

---

## 2. Destination Expansion Dynamics

When users return, do they return only to the same destination or do they expand to new regional clusters?

$$\text{Destination Expansion Rate} = \frac{\text{Unique new regional destinations explored post-first-trip}}{\text{Initial destinations explored}}$$

Validation cohorts in Chhattisgarh reveal:
- Bastar first-timers expand to **Kondagaon craft clusters** in 42% of return sessions.
- Visitors who completed Sirpur heritage day trips expand to **Mainpat or Kanger Valley multi-day circuits** in 36% of return sessions.
