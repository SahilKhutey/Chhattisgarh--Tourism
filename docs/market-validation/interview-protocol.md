# MV2 Interview Protocol: Behavioral Exploration

## Guiding Principles
1. **Never mention CG Tourism OS** until the interview is complete (if at all).
2. **Observe action over opinion**: Ask participants to screen-share or demonstrate their actual planning method.
3. **Trace consequences**: When a participant mentions a difficulty, ask: "What happened as a result of that?"

---

## Section A — Recent Trip Reflection
- "Tell me about the last leisure or exploration trip you took (within Chhattisgarh or similar regional destination)."
- "How did you first pick this location?"
- "Where did you look for information?"
- "What factors did you compare?"
- "What proved unexpectedly difficult or inaccurate?"

## Section B — Regional Discovery
- "Suppose you want to spend an upcoming weekend somewhere in Chhattisgarh you have never visited. What do you do first?"
- Track tools used: Search / Google Maps / Instagram / YouTube / WhatsApp / Blogs / Government Portals / Word of Mouth.
- "What usually makes you choose one destination over another?"
- "How do you decide whether a photo you saw online is realistic?"

## Section C — Information Trust & Verification
- "What specific information do you need before committing to visit?"
  *(Distance, travel time, road conditions, opening hours, ticket fees, weather, crowds, accommodation safety, cell connectivity)*
- "Which of these details is hardest to verify reliably?"
- "Have you ever arrived at a destination in Chhattisgarh and encountered a surprise (e.g., dry waterfall, closed sanctuary, bad road)?"

## Section D — Live Planning Task (Bastar Scenario)
> **Prompt:** "You have three full days and want to explore the Bastar region. You are starting from Raipur. Show me exactly how you would plan this trip right now using your phone or laptop."

### Researcher Observation Sheet:
- **Tools Opened:** Count and sequence (e.g., Chrome -> YouTube -> Maps -> MakeMyTrip -> WhatsApp).
- **Time to First Destination:** Duration in minutes until the participant commits to a first stop.
- **Search Queries:** Record verbatim queries.
- **Workarounds:** Manual notes, screenshots, messaging friends for local driver contacts.
- **Unresolved Questions:** What questions remained unanswered at the end of 20 minutes?
- **Workflow Fragmentation Score:** Total count of distinct apps/tabs used.
