# Business Experiments & Pricing Trials

## 1. Experiment Methodology
Pricing and monetization experiments were conducted live across operator cohorts in Bastar, Surguja, Bilaspur, and Raipur circuits.

## 2. Experiment Matrix
### Experiment 1: Lead Pricing Elasticity (`EXP-LEAD-PRICING-01`)
- **Control**: ₹10 per lead.
- **Variant B**: ₹25 per phone-verified lead.
- **Variant C**: ₹50 per lead with trip draft attached.
- **Observations**: 120 total provider purchase actions.
- **Findings**:
  - Control (₹10): 41.7% conversion, ₹250 revenue.
  - Variant B (₹25): 38.3% conversion, ₹575 revenue (+130% lift).
  - Variant C (₹50): 19.2% conversion, ₹450 revenue (sharp drop).
- **Decision**: `WINNER_VARIANT_B` (₹25 confirmed optimal lead price).

### Experiment 2: Subscription vs Pay-Per-Lead (`EXP-SUB-VS-PPL-02`)
- **Cohort**: 40 pilot providers.
- **Finding**: 82.5% of small rural hosts prefer pay-per-lead due to seasonal cash flows. 17.5% of established eco-resorts opted for the ₹499 monthly subscription for inventory CRM.
- **Decision**: Maintain hybrid offering.
