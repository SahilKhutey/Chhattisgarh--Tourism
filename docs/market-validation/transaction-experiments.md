# Transaction Validation Experiments

## 1. Overview of MV7 Validation Experiments

To validate conversion mechanics empirically, MV7 structures controlled field experiments comparing alternative interaction models.

---

## 2. Active Experiment Designs

### Experiment TX-01: Direct WhatsApp vs. Assisted Form Intake
- **Hypothesis**: Travelers in India prefer direct WhatsApp handoff for inquiries, but assisted forms yield 3x higher lead qualification quality.
- **Variant A (Direct WhatsApp)**: Click-to-chat button opening WhatsApp with pre-filled greeting.
- **Variant B (Assisted Form)**: Structured intake form capturing dates, headcount, and budget band before connecting to operator.
- **Evaluation Metrics**:
  - Top-of-funnel click rate.
  - Lead-to-booking qualification rate.
  - Operator completion rate.

### Experiment TX-02: Instant Quote vs. Clarification Dialogue
- **Hypothesis**: Standardized pricing tiers displayed upfront reduce inquiry abandonment by > 35% compared to "Inquire for custom price".
- **Variant A (Transparent Tier Cards)**: Displaying exact day-rates and seasonal packages.
- **Variant B (Custom Inquiry)**: Open text box for itinerary negotiation.
- **Evaluation Metrics**:
  - Inquiry initiation rate.
  - Provider response turnaround time.

### Experiment TX-03: Pay on Arrival vs. Advance UPI Token
- **Hypothesis**: Requiring a 10% UPI advance token decreases booking cancellation rate from 28% to < 8% without reducing initial intent by more than 15%.
- **Variant A**: 100% Pay on Arrival.
- **Variant B**: 10% direct UPI token reservation to host with proof upload.
- **Evaluation Metrics**:
  - Intent submission volume.
  - Host no-show cancellation rate.
