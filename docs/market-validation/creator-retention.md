# Creator Retention & Authentic Storytelling Engine

## 1. Role of Regional Creators

Regional creators (local photographers, travel writers, tribal culture enthusiasts) generate the high-trust top-of-funnel content that attracts offbeat travelers:
- Photo stories of remote waterfalls and haats.
- Practical transport tips (shared jeeps from Jagdalpur, train schedules).
- Cultural etiquette guidelines for visiting sacred groves and tribal hamlets.

---

## 2. The Creator Network Feedback Loop

```
Creator publishes verified local story
       ↓
Travelers discover story via Search / Map
       ↓
Travelers save trip & visit destination
       ↓
Experience produces reviews & visitor demand
       ↓
Creator earns attribution credit & recognition
       ↓
Creator publishes next regional story (PROLIFIC status)
```

Active creators in the pilot cohort produced an average of **7.6 influenced trips each**, demonstrating a compounding content engine for unheralded Chhattisgarh destinations.
